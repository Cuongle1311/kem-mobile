Privacy Policy

QR Code Scanner Pro – Barcode (Android, package com.scanlab.qrreader) · Published by ScanLab · Last updated: 20 October 2026

QR Code Scanner Pro ("the app") is built to work without an account and without sending your scans anywhere. This page explains exactly what the app does with data, which third-party services it uses, and how to contact us.

1. Summary

  • Camera frames are processed on your device only. They are never stored or uploaded.
  • Your scan history and created codes are stored only on your phone. You can delete them at any time.
  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts.
  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Usage statistics are collected through Firebase Analytics. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

2. Data the app handles on your device

Content you scan (links, Wi-Fi details, contacts, text) is shown to you and acted on only when you tap a button, for example "Open in browser" or "Connect". The app never opens links automatically unless you turn that option on in Settings.

3. Third-party services

The app includes the following services from Google. Each collects data under its own privacy policy, generally device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), coarse device and app information, and interaction events.

  • Google AdMob – shows ads in the free version. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are loaded, and you can change your choice later under Settings → "Privacy options". How Google uses data in ads
  • Firebase Analytics – collects anonymous usage events (for example "a code was scanned", "a code was created", which screens are used) so we can improve the app. These events never contain the content of your codes. Firebase privacy
  • Google Play Billing – processes Premium purchases. We receive only a purchase token that tells us whether Premium is active; we never see your payment details. Google privacy policy
  • Google Play In-App Review – shows Google's rating dialog when you choose to rate the app.

Premium removes all ads. Analytics remains active in Premium so that crashes and feature usage can still be understood.

5. Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores lives on your device. Delete your history from History → ⋮ → "Clear history", or uninstall the app to remove all app data. Data held by Google services is retained according to their policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage Google data at myaccount.google.com.

6. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

The app does not transmit your scanned content. Communication with Google services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects your data, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
